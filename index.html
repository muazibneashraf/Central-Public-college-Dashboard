Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What are the eligibility criteria for college admission?
Students must have completed their secondary certificate from a recognized board. The Science stream requires a minimum GPA of 4.00, Business Studies requires minimum GPA 3.00, and Humanities requires minimum GPA 2.50.
What accreditation and code does the college hold?
The college is fully authorized by the designated secondary & higher secondary education board with institutional accreditation code 131217.
Are merit scholarships or tuition fee waivers available?
Yes! Candidates with Golden GPA 5.00 receive up to a 100% tuition fee merit waiver. Students with GPA 5.00 receive a 50% waiver, and GPA 4.50-4.99 receive a 25% waiver.
What are daily college class hours and attendance policy?
Regular classes operate Sunday through Thursday from 8:00 AM to 1:30 PM. A minimum 85% attendance rate is mandatory for board exam clearance. Science laboratory practicals are conducted in dedicated afternoon cohorts.
How does online admission work?
Candidates can apply via the central online admission gateway or visit the college front counseling desk for direct guidance and document verification.
